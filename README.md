# 📊 CSAT & Revenue Optimization Analysis

## 📌 Executive Summary

This project analyzes customer satisfaction (CSAT) and its direct impact on revenue for a telecommunications company. The analysis identifies key pain points, quantifies their financial impact, and provides actionable recommendations to improve customer experience while maximizing revenue retention.

## 📈 Key Insights

### 1. **Revenue Impact by CSAT Score**
- **Major Concern** customers generate only **€41,706** in revenue
- **Needs Attention** segment contributes **€91,026**
- **Excellent** customers drive **€138,067** - **3.3x more than Major Concern**

### 2. **Critical Customer Issues** (Priority Ranking)
1. **Internet Connection Reliability** - 92 (Critical)
2. **Technical Support Response Time** - 88 (Critical)
3. **Contract Terms & Flexibility** - 85 (High)
4. **Installation Process Complexity** - 78 (Medium)
5. **Billing & Pricing Transparency** - 75 (Medium)

### 3. **Offer Performance Analysis**
#### Revenue Contribution:
- **Freebox Ultra**: €480K (Highest revenue)
- **Freebox Delta**: €360K
- **Box 5G**: €300K
- **Freebox Pop**: €320K

#### Customer Satisfaction (CSAT):
- **Top Performers**: 
  - Série Spéciale Pop S (High CSAT at low price)
  - Freebox Révolution Light
- **Areas for Improvement**:
  - Box 5G (Lowest CSAT despite premium positioning)
  - Freebox Ultra (High revenue but moderate CSAT)

#### Net Promoter Score (NPS):
- **Industry Average**: +35
- **Best Offers**: POP (+55), Rev Light (+48)
- **Needs Attention**: Mini 4K (+22), Box 5G (+18)

1. **Data Collection**: Customer feedback, CSAT scores, revenue data
2. **Sentiment Analysis**: NLP techniques to categorize issues
3. **Impact Scoring**: Frequency vs. Satisfaction impact matrix
4. **ROI Calculation**: Expected return on intervention investments
5. **Priority Framework**: Four-quadrant analysis for decision making
